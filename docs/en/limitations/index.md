# Limitations

## Scope

These are the limitations of the pilot as it currently stands.

## Current Limitations

- Results are conditional on researcher-defined claims and modeling assumptions.
  NIST does not assert the claims.
  See: [Experimental claims](../experiment/10-design/claims.md)
- Independence of organic and adversarial behavior is a researcher assumption.
  See: [Experimental claims](../experiment/10-design/claims.md)
- The finite model treats behavior as directly observable.
  It does not represent sampled prompts, responses, or human annotations.
  See: [Evidence requirements](../experiment/10-design/evidence-requirements.md)
- Evaluator access, trust, cooperation, and resource conditions are not modeled.
  See: [Evaluator conditions](../experiment/10-design/evaluator-contexts.md)
- Realizability of the constructed worlds in operational systems is not established.
  See: [Experimental claims](../experiment/10-design/claims.md)
- Only one example from one framework has an experiment.
- Source-review status is tracked in each mapping record.
  See: [TEVV-001](../mappings/tevv-athlon/TEVV-001.toml)

## Boundaries

The pilot does not establish the safety, trustworthiness, or adequacy of any evaluated system.
See: [Specification](../mappings/SPECIFICATION.md)
