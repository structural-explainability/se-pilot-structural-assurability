# TEVV-Athlon: Claim-Evidence Crosswalk

## Scope

This page covers the two researcher-defined claims
in the query-violation example.
NIST does not assert either claim.

## Claim A: Four Scenarios

Organic use has no violations across four scenarios.
Three are tested in NIST's example, and one is hypothetical.

Evidence examined:
User Testing and Red Teaming in the three tested scenarios.

Result in the model: not resolved.
The untested scenario is unobserved by construction.

## Claim B: Three Tested Scenarios

Organic use has no violations in the three tested scenarios.

Evidence examined: Red Teaming alone.

Result in the model: not resolved,
assuming organic and adversarial behavior vary independently.
Sampled interactions and annotations are not yet modeled.
