# EU Artificial Intelligence Act

## Source

[Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 — Artificial Intelligence Act](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj/eng)

## Potential Investigation

The EU Artificial Intelligence Act is a potential subject for
future Structural Assurability research.

A future investigation would first select specific provisions
and identify their relevant requirements, assessment context,
and applicable version.

The research could then examine whether claim-relative analysis
helps clarify the relationships among assessment obligations,
available evidence, evaluator conditions, and assumptions.

Any such investigation must distinguish the regulation's
requirements from researcher-defined claims and interpretations.
