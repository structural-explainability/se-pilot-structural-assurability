# NIST TEVV-Athlon

## Source

[NIST AI 200-2 ipd: The TEVV-Athlon Framework for Evaluating AI Systems](https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.200-2.ipd)

The TEVV-Athlon Framework provides a flexible method for constructing
AI assessments around organizational evaluation objectives.

Its assessment structure uses measurement Blocks, Events, and Tools
to collect evidence relevant to the characteristics being evaluated.

## Pilot Investigation

The initial Structural Assurability investigation uses NIST's
illustrative query-violation assessment in Section 3.

The research examines relationships among evaluative questions,
available observations, evidentiary capabilities, evaluator conditions,
and assumptions relevant to claim resolution.

Two initial finite-model constructions examine conditional
claim-resolution questions.
Their claims and modeling assumptions are researcher-defined
not defined by NIST.

The investigation also considers what additional evidence or
justification may be needed to connect sampled measurements
to broader evaluative claims.

## Research Status

TEVV-001 remains `OPEN`.

The initial experiment produces reproducible conditional results
within its specified finite models.
